Official Emergency Helplines (India):
  • 112 — National All-in-One Helpline
  • 108 / 102 — Emergency Medical & Ambulance Services
  • 100 — Police Emergency
  • 101 — Fire & Rescue Services
Security & Data Privacy:

GPS telemetry is queried strictly on-demand. No passive background tracking. Consented medical data is snapshotted only into active incident payloads.
